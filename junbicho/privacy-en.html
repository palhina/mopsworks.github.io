3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for the following purposes:

  • Creating, authenticating, and managing user accounts
  • Generating weather-based packing checklists
  • Providing family sharing features for checklists
  • Providing outing history and review features
  • Analyzing usage trends and improving our services
  • Responding to inquiries

If we need to use your personal information for any purpose not listed above, we will obtain your consent in advance.

4. Information Sharing (Family Sharing Feature)

The App includes a family sharing feature for collaborative outing preparation. When using this feature, the following information is shared with members of your family group:

  • Display name
  • Outing information (destination type, date, region)
  • Checklist contents and check states (including who checked each item)
  • Shopping list

Joining a family group requires explicit acceptance through an invitation code. Invitation codes have an expiration period and can only be used once.

Children's information (names, dates of birth) is accessible only to the user who registered them and is never shared with other users, including family members.

6. Data Security

We implement the following measures to protect your information:

  • Data is stored on Supabase (AWS infrastructure), and all communications are protected by SSL/TLS encryption
  • Row Level Security (RLS) ensures that each user's data is accessible only to the user and their family group members
  • Authentication tokens are encrypted with AES-256 and stored locally on your device
  • API keys for weather data retrieval are managed server-side and are not embedded in the app
  • Location data is not permanently stored on our servers and is used only temporarily for weather retrieval

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. In the event of a data breach, we will promptly notify affected users and take appropriate measures.

8. Account Deletion

You can delete your account at any time through the app's settings screen. Upon account deletion, the following data will be permanently erased:

  • User account information (email address, display name)
  • Children's information (names, dates of birth)
  • Outing data, checklists, and review data
  • Shopping list
  • Template customizations
Account deletion is irreversible. A confirmation dialog will be displayed before the deletion is processed.

Anonymized aggregate data collected through Firebase Analytics (statistical information that cannot identify individuals) may be retained for service improvement purposes.

If you are using the family sharing feature, account deletion will remove you from the family group. Outing data created by the deleted user will remain accessible to other family group members.

9. Children's Privacy

This App is designed for parents to use when preparing for outings with their children. The App itself is intended for users aged 13 and above, and we do not expect children under 13 to create accounts or use the App directly.

If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided personal information, we will promptly delete such information. Parents or guardians may contact us at the address below.
